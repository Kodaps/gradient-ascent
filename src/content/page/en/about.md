## How (and when) did Kodaps get started ?

At the start of the 2020 lockdown, there were people in my (David's) team who were wording on digital marketing and who were at a loss as to what to do :
people were no longer interested in what we were selling, and no amount of ads seemed to change that. 

We had a plan to create landing pages, but there wasn't anyone else in the company able to do so other than me.

Together we decided to that I would teach them the basics of HTML and CSS.
