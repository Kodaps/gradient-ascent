I (David) am not a lawyer by any stretch of the imagination, so I could either get "inspired" by someone 
else's privacy policy, or find a boiler plate. Or just tell you in my own words what I will and won't do with 
your data.

* I will only collect data when there is a real need, for example if you ask me to send you something I will store your email
* I will use third party tools like Google Analytics to understand what people are doing on the blog.
* I will not sell your data to any third party
* I will warn you if I detect any data breaches.

My goal with this blog is to educate and share my passion, but also to provide for my family, 
so be aware that by default links to products will be affiliate links, and so have some kind of tracking (depending on the product).
